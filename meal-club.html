HOW TO USE YOUR PLANNER

One millet meal at a time

This is a flexible guide, not a strict diet plan. Keep the rest of your family’s usual meals and choose the suggested millet dish when it suits your routine.

Simple approach: Pick one suggested millet meal each day. Adjust portions, ingredients and meal timing to your household.

How the four weeks work

  1. Week 1 — Breakfast: Try easy morning options.
  2. Week 2 — Lunch: Explore familiar lunch-style dishes.
  3. Week 3 — Dinner: Try comforting dinner combinations.
  4. Week 4 — Family favourites: Repeat, mix and adapt the meals you liked.

Shopping and prep

  • Use the weekly lists as a starting point; quantities depend on family size and how often you cook each dish.
  • Read the package instructions for your specific grain and processing type.
  • Wash, soak or rinse grains when your recipe or pack instructions recommend it.
  • Keep leftovers refrigerated promptly and reheat thoroughly.
No need to follow every day perfectly. Swap days, repeat favourites and use what you already have at home.

PRACTICAL COOKING GUIDE

Make the recipes work for you

Before cooking

  • Check whether your recipe calls for whole millet, flour, flakes or a processed variety.
  • Rinse or soak only as directed by the recipe or package; times differ.
  • Measure the grain and start with the recipe's liquid guidance.

While cooking

  • Water needs differ across millet types and between porridge, pulao, dosa batter and roti.
  • Check texture near the end and add small amounts of hot water if needed.
  • For dough or batter, add liquid gradually and allow it to rest if the recipe recommends it.

Storage and serving

  • Store dry grains in clean, dry, airtight containers away from moisture and pests.
  • Refrigerate cooked food promptly; reheat thoroughly before serving.
  • Serve with familiar accompaniments such as dal, vegetables, curd or chutney according to preference.
Reminder: this planner is for meal inspiration, not medical or nutrition advice. Individual needs vary; seek qualified guidance for special dietary requirements.
